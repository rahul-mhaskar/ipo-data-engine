Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Track My IPO ("we", "our", or "the app"), operated by Opal Technologies, is an initial public offering (IPO) tracking and informational utility tool. We follow a strict local-first architecture to protect user privacy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No User Account: You can use all features without signing up, logging in, or providing personal details like your name, phone number, or email.
  • Zero Cloud Telemetry: We do not track, log, or upload personal application or portfolio data to external servers.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

  • PAN Numbers & Applications: Any Permanent Account Numbers (PANs) or bidding records you save for allotment tracking remain strictly on your local Android device inside secure, private app storage.
  • Encryption: Local data is stored securely on-device and is never transmitted to or shared with Opal Technologies or third-party servers.

3. Market Data Feeds

Market timelines, price bands, issue sizes, and subscription figures displayed in the app are retrieved via read-only requests from public stock exchange filings and market sources.

4. Push Notifications

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to transmit general market alerts (such as bidding opening and closing day reminders). These alerts contain no personal identifiers.

5. Statutory Disclaimer

Track My IPO is an informational utility and is not a SEBI-registered Research Analyst or Investment Adviser. Grey Market Premium (GMP) estimates are crowdsourced indicators provided solely for educational reference and do not guarantee allotment or listing performance.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@opaltechnologies.com.
